# Cortico Health

> Patient engagement software for medical clinics that integrates with EMRs to reduce administrative work and improve patient experience. Also a healthcare directory for patients to find clinics, pharmacies, and laboratories across North America.

Cortico is the healthcare connection platform linking patients, medical clinics, and health records across North America.

Cortico Health Technologies, founded in 2019 in Vancouver BC, automates over 3 million administrative tasks monthly for 6,000+ healthcare professionals across North America. The platform serves two audiences: medical clinics seeking workflow automation, and patients seeking healthcare access. Cortico holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications and is compliant with HIPAA, PIPEDA, and PHIPA.

## For Clinics

- [Online Booking](https://cortico.health/features/patient-scheduling-software/): 24/7 patient self-scheduling integrated with EMR calendars
- [Automated Reminders](https://cortico.health/features/medical-appointment-reminders/): Email and text appointment reminders to reduce no-shows
- [Patient Messaging](https://cortico.health/features/patient-messaging/): Secure two-way communication from within the EMR
- [Intake Forms](https://cortico.health/features/patient-intake-software/): Digital intake forms that sync directly to the EMR
- [Telemedicine](https://cortico.health/features/telemedicine/): Secure video consultations with integrated EMR note-taking
- [Healthcare AI Automations](https://cortico.health/features/healthcare-ai-automations/): Automated patient communication to free up phone lines, plus secure, privacy-compliant clinician LLM access with patient context
- [Request to Book](https://cortico.health/features/request-to-book/): Online specialist booking for referral automation
- [Payment Automation](https://cortico.health/features/healthcare-payment-automation/): Multi-modal private pay, invoicing, and no-show fee collection
- [Patient Tasks](https://cortico.health/features/patient-tasks/): Assign and track patient tasks
- [Clinic Website](https://cortico.health/features/clinic-website/): Turn-key integrated clinic website with booking
- [Digital Signatures](https://cortico.health/features/digital-health-signatures/): Secure document verification
- [Self-Registration](https://cortico.health/features/patient-self-registration/): Let patients update their own demographic details
- [EMR Task Automation](https://cortico.health/features/emr-task-automation/): Automate follow-ups from the EMR
- [Personal Health Records](https://cortico.health/features/personal-health-records/): Patient portal for instant access to files
- [Pricing](https://cortico.health/pricing/): Three plans — Essentials ($86/mo per FTE), Premium ($119/mo), Elite ($199/mo) with 90-day money-back guarantee

## For Patients

- [Patient Portal](https://cortico.health/patients/): Manage appointments, message providers, view test results, and request prescription refills
- [Find a Clinic](https://cortico.health/clinics/): Directory of 94,000+ clinics across Canada and the US, searchable by location and specialty
- [Find a Pharmacy](https://cortico.health/pharmacies/): Pharmacy directory searchable by province
- [Find a Laboratory](https://cortico.health/laboratories/): Laboratory directory searchable by province
- [Specialties](https://cortico.health/specialties/walk-in/): Browse clinics by specialty — walk-in, family doctor, pediatrician, cardiologist, and 60+ more
